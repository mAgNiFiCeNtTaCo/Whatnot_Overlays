Camera & outline
  • Use the same camera, light and sleeves you stream with. That's what it has to learn.
  • Each frame costs OBS a little CPU. If your stream stutters while the scanner is open, lower the frame rate.
  • OBS keeps the camera: pick OBS: <your camera source> and the scanner takes frames straight from OBS (OBS → Tools → WebSocket Server Settings must be on). They come out turned the way your scene turns them.
  • Keys: 1–6 pick the rarity (or answer a card that needs your answer), A auto mode on/off, Space capture, G guess, Esc cancel, D save the frame for tuning when a card isn't picked up.
